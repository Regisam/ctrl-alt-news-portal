## Description

Create dedicated settings page (`/settings`) where users can configure personalization preferences, manage their data, and control their engagement tracking. Allow users to export their data and manage A/B test participation.

**Reference**: EPIC-11 Phase 3: Analytics & Insights  
**Prerequisite**: Stories 11.1, 11.2, 11.3, 11.8 (user data) ✅
