## Story

As a **user**, I want to **see personalized article recommendations** so that **I can discover relevant content without manually searching**.

**Context:**
- No content recommendations
- Users must manually browse or search
- High bounce rate (users leave without exploring)
- Low time-on-site due to navigation friction
- Missed engagement opportunities

- Machine learning models (use heuristics instead)
- Multi-language recommendations
- Social-based recommendations (friend preferences)
- Trending/popularity-based only (too generic)
- Video recommendations
- Long-term offline model training

## Dev Notes

### Blockers
None identified.

### Gotchas
- Cold start problem: new users have no history
- Data sparsity: most users read few articles
- Popular bias: algorithm favors popular articles
- Diversity: avoid filter bubble (only similar content)
- Performance: similarity calculation is O(n²)
- Staleness: recommendations need regular refresh
- Explainability: users want to know why
