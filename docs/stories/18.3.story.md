## Story

As a **user**, I want to **follow other users and authors** so that **I can stay updated with their content and activity**.

**Context:**
- No way to follow users or authors
- Users must manually check for new content
- No personalized content feed
- Missing engagement mechanism
- Low retention due to content discovery friction
