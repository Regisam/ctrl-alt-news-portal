## Story

As a **developer**, I want to **consolidate 3 separate recommendation systems into one unified API** so that **the codebase is simpler and recommendations are consistent**.

**Context:**
- `/api/recommendations` (Story 17.4)
- `/api/topic-recommendations` (Story 13.7)
- `/api/discovery` (Story 19.3)
- Duplicate logic across 3 routers
- No unified caching strategy
- Inconsistent response formats
