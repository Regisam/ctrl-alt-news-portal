## Story

As a **product team**, I want to **refine topic recommendation accuracy and serendipity balance** so that **users consistently discover valuable topics outside their comfort zone while maintaining high adoption rates and engagement**.

**Context:**
- Story 13.7 (Personalized Topic Recommendations) launched the basic ML-driven recommendation engine
- Post-launch analytics show opportunity to optimize:
  - Serendipity score weighting (discovery vs. safety)
  - Cold-start topic diversity (new users getting stale popular topics)
  - Topic embedding quality (better capturing topic affinity)
  - Real Supabase integration (Problema 2) provides production data
- This story refines the algorithm and user experience based on real engagement patterns

## IN Scope

- **Serendipity Score Tuning**:
  - Define λ parameter: balance between recommended topics (collaboration) vs. serendipitous topics
  - A/B test variants: λ = 0.3 (high serendipity), λ = 0.5 (balanced), λ = 0.7 (safer)
  - Measure adoption rate per variant
  - Ship winning variant to all users

- **Topic Embeddings Refinement**:
  - Compute topic embeddings from article vectors in Supabase (real data)
  - Update embedding aggregation: weighted average (articles per topic)
  - Re-rank recommendations using refined embeddings

- **Cold-Start Strategy**:
  - New users: 2 popular topics (trending in category) + 3 serendipitous topics
  - Previously: all 5 from most popular across platform
  - Hypothesis: diversified cold-start increases long-term adoption

- **User Feedback Integration** (optional):
  - Widget: "Was this helpful?" on recommended topics
  - Track feedback (helpful vs. not)
  - Adjust user-topic similarity based on feedback

- **Monitoring & Analytics**:
  - Daily dashboard: CTR per variant, adoption rate, cross-topic engagement
  - Segment by new vs. returning users
  - Detect quality degradation (adoption drop)

- **Tests**:
  - Unit: serendipity weighting function, embedding aggregation
  - Integration: cold-start topic selection, feedback capture
  - A/B analysis: statistical significance of adoption lift
