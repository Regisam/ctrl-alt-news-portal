## Story

As a **user**, I want to **receive smart notifications about interactions** so that **I stay informed without being overwhelmed**.

**Context:**
- No notification system for social interactions
- Users miss replies, new followers, likes
- No way to customize notifications
- Missing engagement signals
- No notification history tracking
