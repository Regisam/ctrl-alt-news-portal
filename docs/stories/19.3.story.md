## Story

As a **user**, I want to **discover new creators based on my interests** so that **I can follow relevant creators and expand my network**.

**Context:**
- No way to discover users
- Limited creator discovery
- Cannot find creators by interest
- Missing personalization in discovery
- No recommendations algorithm
