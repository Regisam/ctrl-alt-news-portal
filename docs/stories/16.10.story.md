## Story

As a **operations team**, I want to **ensure data recovery in case of failure** so that **we can restore the service to a known good state and minimize data loss**.

**Context:**
- No backup strategy in place
- Data loss = unrecoverable
- No recovery procedures documented
- No point-in-time recovery capability
- No disaster recovery testing

## Dev Notes

### Blockers
None identified.

### Gotchas
- Backup size: large databases → large backups → slow transfer
- Verification overhead: restoring to verify is slow/costly
- Encryption keys: loss of key = loss of backups
- Testing: DR drill takes time and resources
- Storage costs: 30 days × daily backups = significant storage
- Recovery validation: how to verify data integrity?
