## Description

Implement trending articles algorithm that combines engagement signals (reactions, bookmarks, shares) with recency weighting. Build rules that rank articles by the intersection of trending topics and user interests: "IF article is trending in user's favorite topic → rank high in recommendations." This story completes EPIC-12 Phase 1 by integrating topic rules (12.1), author rules (12.2), and recency-engagement signals into a unified ranking system.

**Reference**: EPIC-12 Phase 1: Rules Engine Foundation  
**Prerequisite**: Stories 12.1 (topic rules) & 12.2 (author rules) ✅

**Related Stories:**

**Testing Standards:**

- Caching: verify trending results cached and invalidated correctly

**Data Dependencies:**
- Article reactions (from EPIC-11.3, via useReactions hook)
- Article bookmarks (from EPIC-11.2, aggregated counts)
- Article shares (from GA4 events, tracked in EPIC-11.7)
- Article creation date (from article metadata)
- User's favorite categories (from EPIC-11.1, useUserPreferences)
- A/B test variant (from EPIC-11.8, to gate trending rules)

**Formula Notes:**
- Engagement weights: reactions (1x), bookmarks (2x), shares (3x) — shares are highest signal
- Time decay: articles published in last 7 days get 1.5x multiplier, 8-30 days get 1.0x, older get 0.7x
- Trending window: calculate trending for last 7 days, 30 days, all time (configurable)
- Top N: return top 10 articles per category per trending window
