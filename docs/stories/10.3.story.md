## Description

Generate and maintain sitemap.xml files for search engine crawling. Configure robots.txt to guide search engines on which pages to crawl. Ensure all article pages and category pages are discoverable while blocking admin/duplicate pages.

**Reference**: EPIC-10 (Growth & Discovery), Phase 1: SEO Foundation  
**Prerequisite**: Project structure ✅  
**Blocker Status**: SEO foundation requirement
